What to do with your tax refund: a simple plan

Updated November 19, 2026 · 4 min read · For people in Canada

In short: Decide before it arrives. A common order is high-interest debt first, then the emergency fund, then savings goals, with a small slice for something fun. A very large refund every year may mean too much tax is withheld from your pay.

A tax refund feels like free money. It isn't. It's your own money coming back. That's a good reason to give it a plan before it lands.

1. Decide before it arrives

Refunds that arrive without a plan tend to vanish. Write down where each part will go now, while it's still just a number.

2. A simple order many people use

  • High-interest debt first. Paying down a credit card can be one of the best guaranteed "returns" you can get. The debt payoff calculator shows the difference it makes.
  • Then your emergency fund, if it isn't where you want it. See the emergency fund calculator.
  • Then your goals: a TFSA, RRSP or FHSA if they fit your situation, or a sinking fund for a known cost.
  • And a slice for fun. A small, planned treat makes the rest of the plan easier to stick to.

Example (made-up numbers): a $1,500 refund split as $900 to a credit card, $400 to the emergency fund and $200 for something fun.

3. A big refund can be a clue

A large refund every year can mean more tax is being withheld from your pay than you need. It's not wrong, but it means you're waiting a year for your own money. If your situation changed, it may be worth reviewing your TD1 forms with your employer. Check canada.ca or talk to a professional about your situation.

4. Owe instead?

If you owe, pay by the deadline if you can, since interest can apply to unpaid balances. If you can't pay it all, contact the CRA about options rather than ignoring it.

Common questions

What is the smartest thing to do with a tax refund?

There is no single answer. Many people pay down high-interest debt first, then build an emergency fund, then put money toward goals, keeping a small amount for something fun.

Made in Canada, for Canadians

Canadian tax rules

All 13 provinces and territories, with the 2026 brackets and credits. Paycheque estimates are tested against the CRA's payroll formulas.

Data stored in Canada

Your own budget stays on your phone. If you sign in for a household or to sync, your data is stored in Canada.

English and French

The app, this website and the legal texts are in both languages. The app follows your phone's language.

Estimates only

  • MapleBook provides estimates for general information and budgeting only. It is not tax, legal, accounting, financial or investment advice.
  • Results depend on the information you enter and may be inaccurate or incomplete. Your actual taxes and contribution room are set by the CRA (and Revenu Québec in Quebec).
  • Talk to a qualified professional before making tax, investment or registered-account decisions.

Your data stays with you

  • Using MapleBook for your own budget needs no account: your budget stays on your device. Only feedback you choose to send reaches us.
  • If you sign in (needed for households and to sync between your devices), your personal data is stored privately with our hosting provider in Canada. Nobody else can see it, including other members of your household.
  • Only what you put in a household is shared, and only with its members. You choose what they see about your income.
  • Bank statements you import are read on your device. The files are not uploaded to us.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.

What we never do

  • No ads in the app
  • No selling or renting your data
  • No advertising or analytics trackers, in the app or on this website

Questions

Is MapleBook free?

Yes. Everyday budgeting, savings goals and backups are free. MapleBook Plus adds households, statement imports, tax and paycheque estimates and the planning tools for $9.99 CAD a month or $99.99 CAD a year, with a 7-day free trial for first-time subscribers.

Do I need an account?

No. For your own budget everything stays on your phone, with no account. An account is only needed for a household or to sync between devices.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. If you sign in, your data is stored with our hosting provider in Canada. Statements you import are read on your phone and never uploaded.

Is this tax advice?

No. MapleBook shows estimates for budgeting. The CRA (and Revenu Québec in Quebec) sets your actual tax. Talk to a qualified professional before making tax or investment decisions.

Does it work in Quebec?

Yes, with QPP and QPIP. Quebec provincial tax is a simplified estimate.

Which bank statements can I import?

PDF statements with selectable text from TD, RBC, CIBC, BMO, Scotiabank, Tangerine, Simplii, EQ Bank, Desjardins and most credit unions, and CSV, OFX or QFX downloads from most Canadian banks.

Can my partner and I share a budget?

Yes, with a household (up to 6 people). Each transaction is yours or shared; you choose what others see about your income; settle up 50/50, by income or your own way. One subscription covers everyone in the household.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. MapleBook is on Android. You can try every screen in the demo in your browser.

How do I cancel MapleBook Plus?

In Google Play (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → MapleBook Plus) or in the app (Settings → MapleBook Plus → Manage subscription). Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Delete your account

You only have a MapleBook account if you signed in to use a household or to sync between devices. You can delete it and all the MapleBook data stored with it at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Account and sync → Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. You can choose to keep a copy on your phone.
  • By email: write to support@maplebooksoftware.com from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We confirm by email once it's done.

Deleting the account permanently removes your sign-in and your synced personal data, and takes you out of your household (what you shared stays with the household). It doesn't cancel a MapleBook Plus subscription: cancel that in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). Data that is only on your phone is removed when you choose "Delete everything" in Settings or uninstall the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.

8. Households

A household is a shared space for up to 6 people who each have a MapleBook account. The person who creates it is its owner. Anyone with the household's invite code can join until it is full; the owner can change the code and remove members. Any member can leave at any time.

Members can see and change everything shared with the household: shared transactions, bills, savings goals and settle-up payments, and the names of who added or paid them. Your personal budget is never shared. When you leave or are removed, what you shared stays with the household, and you can no longer see it.

Settle-up amounts are arithmetic based on what members enter and the split the household chooses. They are not a bill, a debt collection notice or a legal obligation, and MapleBook does not move money between people.
